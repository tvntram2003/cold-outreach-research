# Cold Outreach Pipeline for B2B SaaS — Research Project

## Why This Topic
100Hires operates in B2B recruitment where cold outreach is the core
acquisition channel. Understanding what actually works in 2024–2025
outbound is directly applicable to the business model.

## Why These Experts
Experts were not selected from generic "top 10" lists. Each person was
evaluated against 3 criteria:
1. **Verifiable results** — third-party case studies, not self-reported only
2. **Active practitioner** — running a real agency or business right now
3. **Distinct angle** — minimal overlap, each expert covers different aspect
   of cold outreach
