# Don't Build Your Personal Brand Around a Topic

**Author:** Patrick Dang  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/patrickdangbusiness  

---

One thing I wish I had done differently when starting my personal 
brand 6 years ago: I wouldn't have built it around a topic.

## What Happened:

**Phase 1:** Niched down on tech sales how-to videos.
- Worked. Made a full-time living.
- Got sick of talking about the same thing over and over.
- Created a whole new YouTube channel focused on NFTs.

**Phase 2:** NFT YouTube channel became one of the top niche channels.
- Again niched into a topic.
- When NFTs died → starting over again in online business space.

## The Real Question:

Will you be talking about the same thing for the rest of your life?
Most likely not.

## What to Do Instead:

Build a personal brand where people follow YOU — not the topic.

**Examples:** GaryVee, Daniel Dalen, George Heaton.
They can sell anything to their audience.
They can hop on any trend — NFTs, AI, whatever comes next —
because they built a brand people connect with personally.

## The 2026 Strategy:

Going all in on building a personal brand so authentically "me" 
that no matter what comes next, the audience stays interested.

**Key insight:** Topic-based brands have expiration dates.
Personality-based brands compound forever.
Build for who you are — not what you currently know.
