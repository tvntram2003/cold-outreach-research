**Date:** 2025-04

---

Spent 6 months stuck on one question: "What should I actually sell?"

Too many ideas. Too many skills. Too many interests.
Brain spiraling: wrong niche, wasted year, not good enough.

## The Solution: Ikigai Framework + ChatGPT

**Ikigai** = Japanese concept for "reason for being."
4 questions:
1. What do you love?
2. What are you good at?
3. What does the world need?
4. What can you be paid for?

Where all four overlap = your Ikigai.

## The Exact Prompt Used:

"You are an expert personal brand and entrepreneurial coach. 
Help me brainstorm what my Ikigai can be based on these variables: 
what I love, what I'm good at, what the world needs, and what I 
can be paid for."

Fill out 3-5 bullet points under each category → hit enter.
ChatGPT outputs 5 business ideas.

**Result:** One idea was almost word-for-word what became Founder X:
"Creator Business Architect — helping people turn their skills into 
personal brands and print money through content, offers, and AI."

## If You Don't Like the First Ideas:

"I don't like these. Give me 5 more ideas focused on X instead of Y."
Keep re-rolling until something clicks.
Not looking for perfection. Looking for direction.

## What Happened Next:

- Knew what content to make
- Knew who to help
- Knew what to sell
- Made first $5,000 within 30 days

**Key insight:** You figure it out by starting.
Ikigai + AI gives you direction. That's all you need.
Most people never start because they wait for the perfect idea.
