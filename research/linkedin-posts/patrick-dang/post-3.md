# Why YouTube is the Cheat Code for Cold Outreach

**Author:** Patrick Dang  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/patrickdangbusiness  

---

Got a cold DM on Instagram from a random guy selling business services.
Ignored it. He followed up every other day — even made 2 custom 
Loom videos. After the 7th follow-up, got on a call with him.

## What Went Through His Head Before Buying:

This is the exact process your ideal clients think through 
when considering buying from YOU.

**Step 1: Who has he helped that's similar to me?**
Didn't need an exact fit — just proof of who he'd worked with.

**Step 2: Show me your YouTube channel**
Didn't care about views or subscribers (had a few hundred).
Needed to see content that:
- Demonstrated expertise
- Showed client case studies and testimonials
- Gave a feel for personality — could I work with him long term?

**Step 3: Send his channel to my network**
Asked if anyone had heard of him or worked with him.
The "legit test" from friends.

## The Decision Framework:

If he checked all 3 boxes:
- Relative success with clients ✓
- Demonstrates he knows his stuff ✓
- Passes the legit test from friends ✓

→ Most likely moving forward.

## The Key Insight:

No matter how you generate leads — ads, outbound, short form content —
**YouTube acts like a bank of sales assets.**

It influences whether someone likes and trusts you BEFORE the call.

You don't need:
- Hundreds of thousands of views
- To compete with big creators

You need:
- An intentional content strategy
- That speaks directly to your ideal clients

**Key insight:** Views don't close deals. Trust does.
YouTube builds trust at scale — even with a few hundred views per video.
