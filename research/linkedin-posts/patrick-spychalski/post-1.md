Clay just launched an Ads integration. The real headline is being missed.

## What Everyone Is Talking About:
Syncing Clay lists directly to ads platforms — saves 5 minutes 
of manual work.

## What Actually Matters: Coverage

**How ads list matching works:**
Uploading lists requires identity-based hash matching.
An encryption system creates a "hash" to avoid uploading 
personal information directly.
The ads platform (LinkedIn or Meta) checks the hash against 
their user base to find the matching profile.
If it can't find it → you can't run ads to them.

**The Problem:**
LinkedIn coverage is usually below 50%.
That means less than half your list can be targeted.

**Clay's Coverage:**
Averages over 60% — a massive improvement.

## Why This Matters:

Higher coverage = more of your list gets targeted = better 
quality ads reaching the right people.

This isn't just a convenience feature.
It's a fundamental improvement in how well your ads actually work.

**Key insight:** The value of Clay's Ads integration isn't 
the time saved — it's the 60%+ match rate vs industry standard 
below 50%. That delta compounds across every campaign you run.
