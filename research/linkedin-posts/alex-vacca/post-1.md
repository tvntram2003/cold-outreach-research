# 100 GTM Rules — One Cheat Sheet

100 GTM rules. One cheat sheet. Skip 6 months of trial and error.

## 1. ICP & Targeting
- One ICP to start. Not three.
- Define by pain, not demographics.
- Stack 5+ signals. One is a guess.
- Score by signal density, not size.

## 2. Outbound Setup
- Never send from your primary domain.
- Warm inboxes 4 weeks before sending.
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC on day one.
- Cap 20-30 per inbox. Scale with more inboxes.

## 3. Cold Email
- Relevance beats personalization.
- Under 80 words. Nobody reads more.
- One CTA. "Worth a conversation?" works.
- A/B test the offer, not the adjectives.

## 4. LinkedIn Content
- Post from the founder, not the company page.
- 3-5x per week. Consistency compounds.
- Narrate the work. Don't manufacture takes.
- Infographics win. Prioritize them.

## 5. LinkedIn Ads
- Ads for familiarity, not conversion.
- Thought-leader ads. 3-5x the CTR.
- Frequency beats reach. 8x > 1x.
- Measure influenced pipeline, not clicks.

## 6. The Flywheel
- Outbound alone is a treadmill.
- Content is air cover. LinkedIn is the page.
- Ads build familiarity before the email lands.
- Every channel feeds the others.

## 7. AI in GTM
- AI replaces tasks, not judgment.
- Feed it your voice + ICP. Then it wins.
- Clay + AI = signal scoring at scale.
- Automate the first mile. Keep the last human.

## 8. Tech Stack
- CRM first. HubSpot, Salesforce, or Attio.
- Clay for enrichment. Apollo for data.
- Instantly or Lemlist for sending.
- Expandi + Taplio + Common Room for LinkedIn.

## 9. Metrics & Attribution
- Revenue closed is the only metric.
- Stop celebrating meetings booked.
- Add "how did you hear about us?" to every form.
- Pipeline coverage: 4-6x, not 3x anymore.

## 10. Mistakes That Kill Pipeline
- More volume is not the answer.
- Don't hire SDRs before the system works.
- Don't fix copy when targeting is broken.
- Fundamentals first. No system = no leverage.

**Key insight:** Every channel feeds the others. 
Outbound alone is a treadmill — build the flywheel.
