AI agents killed the SDR-led playbook. Here's what ColdIQ is betting on for 2026.

## The Data:
- Cold email reply rates: 8.5% → 5% in 3 years
- GTM engineer postings: +205% in 2025
- Gartner: $15T of B2B spend through AI agents by 2028

## 7 Plays for 2026:

**1. Multi-channel over cold email alone**
Cold email alone stopped working. Paired with other channels, 
inbox is still where money lands.
Multi-channel sequences lift response by 287% over single-channel.

**2. Relevance beats personalization**
20 AI personalizations on a bad offer still get ignored.
Clay hit $100M ARR selling relevance infrastructure, not name-drop widgets.

**3. Content is the best follow-up**
- 92% of B2B buyers walk in with a preferred vendor already picked
- 84% of content sharing happens in private channels nobody can attribute
- Your pipeline is being decided in DMs you'll never see
- Content isn't top-of-funnel anymore. It's closing.

**4. AI will talk to AI before any human call**
2 out of 3 B2B buyers already use AI agents to research vendors.
Your SDR is pitching a robot.

**5. Sell the simple version before the full system**
Clay needs 4-6 weeks of onboarding.
11x AI SDR churned 70-80% of customers.
Companies can't operate the tools they buy.
That's why the $90B outbound services market keeps growing.

**6. Hire a GTM department, not sales + marketing**
Anthropic does $5.6M revenue per employee with one integrated GTM function.
Separate marketing + sales + CS teams won't work anymore.

**7. Trigger on signals. Stop working lists.**
Signals: hiring, funding, product usage, G2 intent, webinar attendance,
content engagement.
At ColdIQ, every outreach is signal-triggered — never a cold list.
One GTM engineer on this motion outbooks a full SDR team on cold ICPs.

**Key insight:** The $90B outbound services market keeps growing because
companies can't operate the tools they buy. Simplicity wins.
