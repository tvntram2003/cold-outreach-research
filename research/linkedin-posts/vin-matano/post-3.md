# I Booked a Last Minute Flight to SXSW to See If It's Worth the Hype

**Author:** Vin Matano  
**Date:** 2025  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/vinmatano  
**Topic:** Community / showing up / B2B event culture

---

I booked a last minute flight to SXSW to see if it's worth the hype. And here's how it went down 👇

Every flight to Austin was crazy expensive, so I had to fly into San Antonio instead. Seventy miles away. I had no badge. No place to stay. Completely unprepared.

But sometimes, showing up unlocks possibilities you never saw coming.

Luckily my friend Jake Clare let me crash on his couch for the weekend. Then I got into JBL Professional's branded event with live music and giant headphones. Manychat's insane creator panel and DJ set by Kyle Cook. Epidemic Sound's rooftop party with a Swedish band. And Later's massive party inside of a pool — they even had a display featuring some of their top client campaigns, which I thought was super unique.

But just when I thought it wasn't possible, the weekend got even more spontaneous.

I randomly stumbled into an intimate album release party with the All-American Rejects. Got interviewed in a pedicab by Kayla Monis. And somehow ended up at a BigxthePlug concert.

Here's what I learned: Most people wait for the perfect plan, the right connections, the ideal timing. But the biggest opportunities come from just showing up.

Because sometimes the best strategy is buying the plane ticket first and figuring out the rest later.

So yeah, I'd say SXSW was worth it 🤝
