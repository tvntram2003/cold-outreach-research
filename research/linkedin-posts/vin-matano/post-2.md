Everyone's spending thousands on Facebook and Google ads, but there's one advertising channel that's completely flying under the radar… CTV.

While everyone's fighting for expensive clicks on social media, smart marketers are getting premium TV placements for a fraction of the cost.

Like Arbor. They wanted to reach buyers in just three cities — Columbus, Dayton, and Pittsburgh. With a tight marketing budget, they used Roku Ads Manager and reached 153,000 unique households at just 5 cents per unique reach.

But here's the crazy part — Arbor's ads let viewers press OK on their remote to request a text message directly from the company. No app downloads, no website visits — just instant engagement from the couch.

Roku Ads Manager makes it as easy as running Facebook ads.
