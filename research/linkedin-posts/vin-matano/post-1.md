# Here's All the Cool Sh*t I Saw in B2B Marketing Last Week

**Author:** Vin Matano  
**Date:** 2025  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/vinmatano  
**Topic:** B2B marketing activations / creative campaigns

Here's all the cool sh*t I saw in B2B Marketing last week 👇

First up, Legora making Jude Law their spokesperson. With a name like 'Jude Law', this one is pretty self explanatory to partner with a Legal tech company. Simple, but effective.

Next up, the Brex Coachella activation. Now, this one is a huge deal as B2B companies are not typically present at Coachella. Brex partnered with Revolve during their REVOLVE Fest and hosted a Brex Cafe serving mini hot dogs topped with caviar. I predict next year will be the year of B2B Coachella activations.

And last but not least, Clay's recent campaign to promote their 'Functions' feature. They partnered with Mark Iacono, owner of the famous NYC pizzeria, Lucali's. Mark explained how the recipe never changes for his famous pizza dough, and you shouldn't have to rebuild in Clay either. Given that Clay is a NY-based company, this felt very on-brand.

And that's all the cool sh*t I saw in B2B Marketing. What else did I miss?
