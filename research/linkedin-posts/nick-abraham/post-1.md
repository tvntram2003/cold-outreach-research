# Fix Your Offer Before Using Claude for Cold Email

**Author:** Nick Abraham  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/nick-abraham  

---

If you've saved ANY "Claude for cold email" post, please read this.

AI makes less than 1% difference if your offer isn't working first.
The most important thing to fix before trying AI is your **offer**.

## 4 Levers to Improve Your Offer:

**Lever 1 — Add a real guarantee**
Not "100% customer satisfaction guaranteed" — everyone says that.
Must be specific and tangible.
Ex: "Funded in 24 hours or your money back."

**Lever 2 — Lead with your structural differentiator**
Leadbird was one of the first pay-per-lead agencies.
Every time they put that in the message, campaigns went nuclear.
If you do something competitors don't + it has tangible output → 
that goes in the email. Not years in business or team size.

**Lever 3 — Make the offer feel like zero risk**
Remove friction and perceived downside simultaneously.
Ex: "Funded in under 24 hours with no credit checks" hits both.

**Lever 4 — Give something away for free to get them on a call**
- Free lead list just for attending a demo
- Enrich their CRM with phone numbers at no cost

Once you do these things, layering on AI responsibly is a great 
way to scale outbound. But you cannot skip this part.

**Key insight:** Offer quality > AI personalization. Always.
