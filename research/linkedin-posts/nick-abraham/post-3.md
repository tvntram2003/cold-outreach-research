# AI Lead Lists Are Pulling the Wrong People — Here's the Fix

**Author:** Nick Abraham  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/nick-abraham  

---

If you're using AI to pull lead lists, there's a good chance you're 
emailing the wrong people and have no idea.

Leadbird sends millions of cold emails/month for 300+ clients.
When they started using MCPs to pull lists from lead databases — 
output looked fine. But the problem runs deeper.

## The Problem: Database Persona Mapping is Often Wrong

Lead databases assign personas based on job title — and that mapping 
is frequently incorrect.

**Example 1:** A content manager at a 300-person company gets bucketed 
as "marketing leader." That person has zero buying power, but the MCP 
pulls them in because the database said they qualified.

**Example 2 — Stacked titles:** Someone lists "Managing Director, CEO, 
Head of SEO" on LinkedIn. Your CEO filter catches them. In reality they 
run a small SEO shop and gave themselves multiple titles.

All of this happens before you send a single email.
You're burning credits and time on people who were never a fit.

## The Fix: Work Off Actual Title Data

Built a custom endpoint in their database:
1. Upload domains from Discolike
2. Pull every raw title across those companies
3. Let AI evaluate which titles are actually relevant to the offer

Instead of relying on whatever persona bucket the database assigned —
work off actual title data directly.

Result: **Bigger lists, way more accurate.**

## Key Takeaway

If you're running outbound with MCPs, look at the lists before you 
push them anywhere. AI will always give you an output — but that 
doesn't mean it's right.
