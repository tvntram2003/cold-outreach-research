# The Only Vendor That Holds Us Accountable

**Author:** Jason Bay  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jasondbay  

---

Best feedback you can get from a client:
"Thank you for being the only vendor that holds us accountable."

Outbound pipegen is a commitment — especially shifting from 
inbound to outbound-led. One of the hardest things a sales org 
can do. Failure rate is high.

You can't just:
- Talk about outbound
- Run a few enablement sessions
- Measure results in a quarter

## What It Actually Takes to Make Outbound Work:

- Commitment from senior leaders to PARTICIPATE
- Buy-in from front-line managers
- A common methodology for everyone to follow
- Clear disqualification criteria to avoid wasting time on 
  poor-fit accounts
- A world-class offer that makes taking a meeting a no-brainer
- Problem-centric messaging that provokes buyers into action
- Great data: accurate phone numbers and emails
- Reps who are properly trained and up-skilled
- Managers committed to reinforcement
- Sales math that calculates activity down to the day to hit 
  pipegen targets

## The Biggest Missed Opportunity:

The phone. AI can't do it for you.

**Key insight:** It takes a village to make outbound work.
But it's far from dead.
