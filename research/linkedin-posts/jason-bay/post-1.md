# The Best Cold Caller I've Ever Met Breaks All the Rules

**Author:** Jason Bay  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jasondbay  

---

The best cold caller Jason has ever met asks "How are you doing today?" 
at the beginning of every call — and books 30%+ of connected calls 
into meetings.

## 4 "Rules" That Don't Actually Apply in Real Life:

**Rule 1: Don't ask "How are you doing?"**
Best cold calling client asked this every call.
Felt it was rude not to. Booked 30%+ of connected calls.
Sometimes you just need to open the way you feel most confident.

**Rule 2: Don't use filler words or umms and uhhs**
A rep who sounds lost in first few seconds — "Is this...uh...John?"
Sounds terrible. Works really well for him anyway.

**Rule 3: Don't monologue**
Misconception: buyer should talk more than you.
Reality: Best reps talk 60%+ of the time on cold calls.
They drive the conversation — not pitch-slapping, but not letting 
the buyer drive a call they never asked to receive.

**Rule 4: Don't pitch**
Industry somehow decided you shouldn't pitch.
But: pitch when you know what they care about.
When buyer shares a problem you can solve → absolutely let them 
know you can help.
Some of the best cold callers pitch early in the call.

## Key Insight:

Data and best practices are guidelines — not laws.
What matters most is what you execute with confidence.
