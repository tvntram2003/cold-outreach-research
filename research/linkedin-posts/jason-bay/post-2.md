# Outbound Isn't Dead — You Just Don't Put in the Work

**Author:** Jason Bay  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jasondbay  

---

Outbound isn't dead. It's not working because most sales orgs 
treat it like an afterthought.

## The Imbalance:

**What orgs invest in the sales process:**
- 7-figure investments in sales methodology
- Clear-cut stages and process
- CRM, call recording, every software imaginable
- Weekly deal reviews
- Formal qualification frameworks like MEDDICC
- Quarterly enablement focuses
- Senior leadership involvement in deals
- Marketing branded slide decks
- Flying to meet customers in person

**What orgs invest in outbound:**
- A bad email sequence built by marketing
- A generic cold call opener from a course
- A subscription to Claude and a sales engagement tool (maybe)

Most managers don't even talk to AEs about pipegen in 1:1s.

## 3 Core Areas to Fix:

**1. Lack of standardized approach or methodology**
Challenger is not an outbound methodology.
Outbound isn't a series of random cold call openers and email templates.
Best orgs strategically approach account selection, messaging, 
tooling, plays — everyone rowing in the same direction.

**2. Little to no tactical "how to" guidance**
Too much pie-in-the-sky theory. Zero examples of how to do it.
Don't tell me to use empathy to handle objections. 
SHOW ME AN EXAMPLE.
Best orgs get extremely hands on.
BDRs shouldn't attend the same enablement sessions as AEs.

**3. Little to no leadership engagement**
Pipeline is an org-wide responsibility — not just the reps.
Can't expect reps to make calls if you aren't willing to do it yourself.
Can't complain about pipeline if you've never listened to a recording 
of your rep's cold calls.
Best orgs: senior leaders "go and see" for themselves.
It starts at the top.

**Key insight:** Outbound requires a village to work.
Put in the work, and it'll work.
