Max rebuilt his entire lead gen department on OpenClaw. Then he ditched the whole setup and moved to Hermes Agent. Here's why 👇

A month ago, Max Mitcham (founder of Trigify.io) showed me his OpenClaw mission control. It went viral. My whole team was sending me the video, saying "it was the craziest setup they'd ever seen".

Two weeks later, Max messaged me: "I'm not using OpenClaw anymore. I'm all in on Hermes Agent."

So we jumped on another call to unpack why:

**1️⃣ OpenClaw breaks a lot**  
If you're not deeply technical, OpenClaw requires constant fixing, wiring, and updates. Hermes works straight out of the box. No debugging, no rewiring every time a new release drops. "I've never had to fix it. Not once."

**2️⃣ Hermes has a built-in memory system**  
Unlike OpenClaw, Hermes has a self-learning loop baked in:
- Auto-creates skills
- Auto-creates memory files
- Auto-updates them based on how you use it

Example: if Max keeps asking for stats/numbers in his LinkedIn hooks, Hermes remembers, updates preferences, and improves for the next LinkedIn posts without Max needing to say "remember this." The more he uses it, the smarter it becomes.

**3️⃣ You don't lose your OpenClaw setup**  
Hermes asks if you want to port your OpenClaw config across on setup. Max didn't even stop using OpenClaw — he just made Hermes the orchestrator and kept OpenClaw for tool-heavy jobs.

New architecture:
- Hermes = architect (90% of work)
- OpenClaw = tool-heavy execution (10%)

**4️⃣ He also ditched Claude**  
Anthropic banned 3rd-party tools from the Max plan. He moved to Codex on the $200 plan, and inside Hermes, he can't tell the difference between Opus 4.6 and GPT 5.4.

**5️⃣ The memory layer is Obsidian**  
Inspired by Andrej Karpathy's post on agent memory, Max structures Hermes's brain like a Wikipedia graph. Every concept links to every other concept. Founder-led content connects to signal-based selling, which connects to brand intelligence. It's not a folder — it's a neural network of markdown files. Hermes reads patterns across the whole graph before answering anything.
