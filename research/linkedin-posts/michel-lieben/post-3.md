We rebuilt our entire content engine with Claude Code. +$151K MRR added in 87 days with 20+ people posting.

It started as a random experiment with 1, then 2, then 3 people posting. Then Mai-Lan Khong built it into a replicable working system:

**▶️ The Foundation** (built once per person):
- Voice profile — 25-question conversation, not a form. How someone talks before AI touches anything.
- ICP document — 3 buyer tiers, mapped to the language each tier uses to search for solutions.
- Content pillars — 3 to 5 themes every post traces back to. Many people skip this and go straight to writing. Then wonder why everything sounds the same.

**▶️ The Research Layer** (runs weekly):
- Apify browses LinkedIn for what's performing in your niche right now
- Reddit for real audience pain language
- YouTube for frameworks worth adapting
- X/Twitter for live debates
- Fireflies.ai transcripts from client calls (the highest-signal source)

Six workers in parallel.

**▶️ The Production Line:**
- Hook generator — 50 templates, 20+ variations per idea
- Copy developer — full draft in your documented voice
- Post grader — 5-dimension rubric, /50. Below 38? Back to rewrite.
- Gemini for visuals. Figma + Canva for refinement. AI drafts. You sharpen.

The voice profile is the guardrail.

**▶️ Repurpose** (one post, many formats):
One LinkedIn post that performed well = one validated idea. Restructured into X/Twitter thread, newsletter, blog, video script, carousel. Each format rebuilt for how people consume on that platform. Repurposing is not copy-pasting — it is restructuring.

**▶️ Refresh + Maintain:**
- Feedback capture logs every session automatically
- Pattern recognition every 5 sessions: what keeps getting rewritten, where voice drifts
- Monthly voice refresh: pull new transcripts, compare against voice profile
- Content audit quarterly: are your pillars still relevant to where the market moved?

The system gets smarter the longer it runs.

**▶️ Delivery:**
ClickUp for client handoff → Taplio for scheduling → Performance data fed back into Claude for the next cycle.

One terminal. 27 skills. Each does one job.

Research → ideas → hooks → copy → grade → repurpose → refresh → deliver

Your folder structure is your content brain. This is how 24 people sound like themselves, at scale.
