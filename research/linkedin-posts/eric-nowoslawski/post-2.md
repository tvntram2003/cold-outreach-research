# 29 Claude Skills for Automated Cold Email Campaign

I put 29 Claude skills into a repository so anyone can launch a cold email 
campaign automatically that ALSO self improves.

Built with 6 months of daily Claude Code usage — 2M lines of code written 
in first 45 days alone.

## What's in the repo (29 skills across 5 tracks):

**Track 1 — Strategy (before you send anything)**
- `cold-email-kickoff` — single "start here" orchestrator
- `icp-onboarding` — scrapes your website, asks 12 questions to lock ICP
- `lead-magnet-brainstorm` — figure out what to offer for free
- `campaign-strategy` — generates 15-25 campaign ideas with AI
- `campaign-copywriting` — stepwise copy writer

**Track 2 — Infrastructure**
- `zapmail-domain-setup` — buy domains on Dynadot, provision on Zapmail
- `smartlead-inbox-manager` — warmup settings, signatures
- `email-deliverability-audit` — SPF/DKIM/DMARC, spam placement
- `deliverability-incident-response` — triage for spam, bounces, blacklists

**Track 3 — List Building**
- `prospeo-full-export` — title-first lead search (25K+)
- `blitz-list-builder` — domain-first contact discovery
- `google-maps-list-builder` — scrape Google Maps for local SMB lists
- `disco-like` — lookalike company discovery
- `competitor-engagers` — find people engaging with competitor LinkedIn posts
- `list-quality-scorecard` — grade a lead CSV across 8 dimensions

**Key hacks:**
- Uses Claude/Codex usage to AI personalize emails instead of paying API tokens
- Skill #30 trained on 90-minute presentation about favorite APIs
- Skill analyzes all campaigns from past week and suggests improvements 
  based on positive reply rate

**Why this matters:** Full end-to-end cold email system that self-improves 
over time — from ICP definition to campaign analysis.
