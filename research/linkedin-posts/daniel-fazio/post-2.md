# Everything in Business Operates on a Contingency System

**Author:** Daniel Fazio  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/coldemailwizard  

---

Every business operates on a contingency system — to do one step, 
you must have already done the previous step.

## Cold Email Example — 8 Steps:
1. Buying the domains
2. Setting up the inboxes
3. Building the lead lists
4. Connecting inboxes to a sending tool
5. Writing scripts
6. Setting up the campaigns
7. Managing the replies
8. Taking the sales calls

Each step is contingent on the previous one being done.

## The Reason a Business Stalls:

One of the steps stops. That's it.

## 4 Ways to Complete Any Step:
1. You do it
2. Somebody else does it
3. Automation
4. AI (autonomous cognition)

## The System of Systems:

To make money:
1. Must fulfill for a client
2. To fulfill → must have closed the client in sales process
3. To close → must have generated them as a lead in marketing

Marketing → Sales → Fulfillment = itself a contingency system.
Each one comprised of its own individual contingency systems.

## Where People Fail:

Your business = 30-50 different boxes of things that need to be 
done every day.

People fail because they stop doing 4-5 boxes they don't like.
The entire system halts because everything is contingent on 
those things being done.

## Why AI Changes Everything:

Previously, anything requiring cognition needed a human.
Now you have a computer doing human-level thinking for a few cents.

This makes scaling way easier and less expensive.

**Key insight:** Don't ask "what should I work on?"
Ask "which box in my contingency system is currently broken?"
