Student was struggling to sign clients with cold email.
Asked her to send the script and lead list.
Dumped everything into Claude. Fixed in 10 seconds.

## Case Study 1: Wrong Decision Makers

**Problem:** Emailing property managers, regional managers, 
assistant managers — nobody with buying power.
Nobody was responding because no relevant people were getting emails.

**Fix:** New list with Founders/Owners only.
**Result:** 3 days later — leads and calls booked.

## Case Study 2: Wrong Companies Entirely

**Problem:** Selling Amazon FBA agency offer, emailing "ecommerce brands."
Response rate terrible. Student thought he needed a new offer.

**Test:** Opened 10 random leads from the list live on coaching call.
**Result:** 0/10 were actually ecommerce brands.

Entire list was irrelevant — which led him to incorrectly think 
he needed to change his entire business offer.

## The 2 Lessons:

1. Stop making obvious mistakes. Wrong people = zero responses.
   Has nothing to do with copy or offer.

2. Use AI to diagnose problems. AI is your hyper-intelligent 
   assistant that can do anything.

## The Exact Prompt That Fixed It in 10 Seconds:

"All of these are documents in regards to a cold email campaign 
that is not converting. Go through the leads and make sure they're 
relevant, then also all of the other docs including the scripts 
we're sending, and try to help me determine what the problem is 
and why the cold emails are not converting. Just give me a 
straightforward solution of what is wrong with the campaign 
and why it's not converting."

Cost: 1.2 cents.
Time: 10 seconds.

## Same Process Works For:
- Video Sales Letters
- Ad Scripts
- Sales Pages
- Dream 100 Outreach Messages
- YouTube Videos
- Short Form Videos

**Key insight:** You don't need to know what's wrong.
You need to know HOW to figure out what's wrong.
Throw all the data in. Give context. Get the answer.
