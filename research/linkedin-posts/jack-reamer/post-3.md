# Stop Trying to Figure Out LinkedIn's Algorithm

**Author:** Jack Reamer  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jackreamer  

---

A post written in 6 minutes on a Friday afternoon — one that was 
almost trashed — was the one LinkedIn decided to show to a 
relatively wide audience.

## The Takeaway:

**Post only to share notes on what you're learning.**

Let go of LinkedIn content as a lead source — for a while.
But stay consistent.

## What Happens When You Do:

- Your writing will improve
- Ideas will flow more easily
- You'll save yourself stress wondering why certain posts 
  "should have" done better

**Key insight:** The best LinkedIn content comes from 
genuine sharing — not from reverse-engineering the algorithm.
Consistency compounds. Optimization is a distraction early on.
