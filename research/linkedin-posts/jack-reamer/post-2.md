# LinkedIn Content Creates Signal — Outreach Converts It

**Author:** Jack Reamer  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jackreamer  

---

"Once I get 25K+ impressions a week, inbound LI leads will start 
booking calls with me on autopilot."

**Myth busted.**

## What Actually Happens:

**A) LinkedIn content matters — but it's a marathon**
An impression is just an impression.
The magic is building recognition, reciprocity, and trust over time.
Not instant inbound leads.

**B) If you want more calls NOW — don't wait for inbound**

Instead:
1. Review who's engaging with your content
2. Filter for people who match your ICP
3. Reach out first

## The Outreach Template That Works:

"Thanks for checking out the post. 
{Personal note on their company/industry/role/posts}
Anything I can help with when it comes to {solving pain}?"

Some teams have success just picking up the phone and calling 
this short list directly.

## The Formula:

**Content creates the signal. Outreach converts it.**

**Key insight:** Don't wait for inbound to come to you.
Use content engagement as a warm signal trigger —
then reach out while you're already top of mind.
