# Emotion Makes Decisions — Not Logic

**Author:** Jack Reamer  
**Date:** 2025-04  
**Profile:** https://linkedin.com/in/jackreamer  

---

Story: Woke up at 4am. 3-year-old son had unlocked the front door 
and was outside in the dark, smiling through the window.

This morning: Son wasn't in his bed. Searched everywhere. Panicked.
"A mix of saliva and rust" — the taste of fear.
All day's priorities immediately and violently shifted.
Then he came out from behind the bathroom door. "Surpirise!"
Dropped to his knees and cried.

## What This Has to Do with Your Sales Strategy:

**Value explains decisions. Emotion makes them.**

Most cold email copy is under-emotional.
If someone looked at your copy right now — you're probably not 
making your prospects feel enough to engage.

Even your most analytical prospects:
- Engineers
- CFOs
- "Show me the data!" types

They're still people. There's still a person behind every 
transaction who wants to feel something:
- Confidence
- Relief  
- Excitement
- The fear of getting it wrong

## The Fix:

Before you hit send, ask yourself:

**"How will this message make my prospect feel?"**

**Key insight:** Emotion in outreach isn't manipulation —
it's acknowledgment that a human is reading your email.
Under-using emotion is one of the most common and costly 
mistakes in cold outreach copy.
